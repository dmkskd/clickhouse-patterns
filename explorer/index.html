Compare runnable patterns, understand their trade-offs, and adapt them for your own systems.

This catalog is a work in progress and the patterns are still under review.

In most ClickHouse systems, querying is rarely the main challenge. The effort goes into how data arrives, how it is modelled and kept current, and how it is delivered, replayed, and spread across a cluster. This collection gathers runnable patterns for those problems, so their trade-offs can be compared on working examples.

Explore existing patterns or create your own to prototype a feature quickly or document an existing system. Clone a pattern as a starting point, then use the agentic skills to define its flow and Docker-based test infrastructure.

        Clone this pattern

        Cloning copies a pattern into workspace-patterns/ under a chosen name, so its schema, load, and checks can be adapted to a specific system without touching the curated original.

        From the repository:

        just clone <pattern> <new-name>

        The copy appears in the Workspace group here, runs the same way with just test <new-name>, and can be removed with just delete <new-name>.
